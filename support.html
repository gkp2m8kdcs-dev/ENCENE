Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use ENCENE?

No. ENCENE is designed to work without an account. All your data is stored locally on your device.

Where is my portfolio data stored?

Your transactions, watchlist, notes, and preferences are stored locally in the app's sandbox on your device. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, an encrypted backup copy may be stored in your personal iCloud account — but it is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours, and we cannot access it.

How do I back up my data?

Your data is automatically included in your standard iOS device backup (iCloud or iTunes/Finder), provided those backups are enabled in iOS Settings. To export an individual trade entry, open it and use the iOS share sheet.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Settings → Data Management inside the app. You can clear all transactions, clear the watchlist, or perform a full reset. You can also simply delete the app from your device, which removes all app-stored data.

Is the market data real-time?

No. Stock prices, charts, and reference data shown in ENCENE are generated locally for educational and demonstration purposes. They do not reflect real-time prices. Always verify current prices through your broker before trading.

Which markets and currencies are supported?

ENCENE is designed for U.S. and Hong Kong equity markets. You can display values in 8 base currencies: USD, HKD, CNY, EUR, GBP, JPY, SGD, and AUD.

Why does the app request Camera or Photo Library access?

Only when you choose to attach a photo to a trade journal entry. The app will prompt you for the specific permission at that moment. You can deny it and still use every other feature.

Does ENCENE provide financial advice?

No. ENCENE is a tracking and journaling tool. It does not provide investment, financial, legal, or tax advice. All investment decisions are your own responsibility.

Quick troubleshooting

  • Chart not loading: Pull-to-refresh on the stock detail screen. If the issue persists, restart the app.
  • P&L looks off: Check your base currency and cost-basis method in Settings.
  • App feels slow after many trades: Use Settings → Data Management to archive or clear old completed transactions.
  • Photo won't attach: Verify that Camera and Photo Library access are granted in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
