Privacy Policy

ENCENE is built around a simple idea: your portfolio is your business. This policy explains exactly what data we collect (spoiler: almost none), how it's stored, and your rights.

Last updated: January 2026

Short version: ENCENE does not require an account, does not collect personal data, and does not transmit your portfolio information to any server. All your trades, watchlist items, photos, and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can wipe everything from inside the app at any time.

1. Information We Collect

ENCENE is designed to operate without collecting personal information from you. Specifically:

1.1 Personal data

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, government identifiers, or any other personal data through normal use of the app. The app does not require you to create an account.

1.2 Usage and analytics data

We do not use third-party analytics services, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels inside ENCENE. We do not log your activity, sessions, device identifiers, IP addresses, or any behavioural data on our servers.

1.3 Content you create

All content you create in ENCENE — transactions, watchlists, notes, photos, and preferences — is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework. This data is never uploaded to our servers or any third-party service.

2. Permissions We Request

ENCENE requests only the device permissions that are strictly necessary to provide features you choose to use. Each permission is requested at the moment it is needed, never silently at first launch.

2.1 Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription)

Used only when you choose to attach a photo to a trade journal entry. Photos are saved into the app's private storage and never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them using iOS system features.

2.2 Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription)

Used only when you choose to attach an existing photo from your library to a trade entry. The app reads only the images you select.

2.3 Photo Library Add (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription)

Used only when you explicitly choose to export a generated chart or report to your Photos library.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time from iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

3. Market Data

Stock prices, charts, and reference data displayed in ENCENE are generated locally for educational and demonstration purposes. The app does not stream live market data from third-party providers. Any market data shown should not be relied upon for actual investment decisions — always verify current prices through your broker.

4. Third-Party Services

ENCENE does not integrate third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, social features, or crash reporting. The only network requests the app makes, if any, are standard Apple system services required for App Store distribution, in-app updates, or iCloud Backup of your local data (which is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours).

5. Children's Privacy

ENCENE is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we collect no personal information at all, this policy applies uniformly.

6. Data Security

Your data is protected by iOS's built-in sandbox and, if enabled on your device, the device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. Because the data never leaves your device, the surface area for unauthorised access is limited to physical access to an unlocked device. If your device is lost or stolen, you can remotely wipe it using Apple's Find My service.

7. Your Rights and Controls

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Access & export: All your data is visible inside the app. Trade entries, notes, and journal attachments can be reviewed and exported via standard iOS share sheets.
  • Delete individual items: Swipe to delete any transaction, watchlist entry, or photo attachment.
  • Bulk delete: Use Settings → Data Management to clear all transactions, clear the watchlist, or reset everything.
  • Uninstall the app: Removing ENCENE from your device deletes all locally stored data created by the app.

8. International Users

Because ENCENE does not transfer your personal data to any server, there is no cross-border data flow to govern. Your data remains within the iOS sandbox on the device you use, in whatever country you reside.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy in a material way, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, surface a notice inside the app or on this page. Because the app does not collect personal data, most changes will be clarifications rather than expansions of data collection.
